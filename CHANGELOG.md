## 0.5.0 (2026-11-20)

Baseline release: Milestones 1 to 5.

### Features

* **Milestone 5:** FBR Digital Invoicing (PRAL) for Pakka bills, with a sandbox mode, a QR code on the bill and a retry button, plus one-click industry blueprints (Foundry, Mandi, Importer, Sanitary) with Pakistani tax templates.
* **Milestone 4:** Kacha / Pakka dual billing and WhatsApp alerts.
* **Branding:** GICNEXT branding; admin areas hidden from GIC roles.
* **Milestone 3:** foundry burning loss, thekedar work log and GIC role permissions.
* **Milestone 2:** munshi-friendly desk, local units and a form simplifier.
* **Milestone 1:** GIC Industry Profile switchboard and fixtures.
